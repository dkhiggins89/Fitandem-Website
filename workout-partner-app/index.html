Built for two

A workout partner app built for two.

You don't need a big group chat or a training buddy who does the exact same workouts. Fitandem is built around one Tandem — a friend, partner or family member — so you can stay connected without matching anyone's routine.

See how it works ↓
  1. Log a move

    Any move counts — yours doesn't have to match theirs.

    Choose a movement category, log the duration and effort that suit you, and see the momentum you're building with your Tandem. Your partner might lift weights while you go for a walk — both moves count.

  2. See shared progress

    Watch your week add up, side by side.

    Your Progress screen tracks weekly minutes and an activity graph, so you can see how your week is going alongside your Tandem's momentum — without needing to train together.

Frequently asked

A few useful things to know.

Quick answers about exercising with a workout partner in Fitandem.

Does my workout partner need to do the same exercise as me?

No. Fitandem tracks each person's own moves — categories, duration and effort — so a run and a strength session on the same day both count toward your Shared Streak.

Can I connect with more than one person?

Fitandem is designed around staying active with people you know, using Tandems to share progress, streaks and encouragement.

Do we need to be free at the same time to exercise?

No. You each log your own move whenever it suits you — the streak only needs both of you to move on the same day, not at the same time.

Is a workout partner required to use Fitandem?

No, you can use Fitandem on your own to log movement and track progress, but Tandems, Shared Streak and nudges only work once you've connected with one other person.
