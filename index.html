FAQ

Common questions.

Is Fitandem free?

Yes. Tandems, the Shared Streak, logging, progress, achievements, nudges and the Workout Finder are all free. Fitandem Duo is an optional subscription for advanced insights, challenges, full history and Shared Streak Rescue.

How does a Shared Streak work?

A Shared Streak grows when both people in a Tandem log a move on the same day. It doesn't matter whether you move together or apart, at 6am or 9pm.

What counts as a move?

Any movement you choose to log. Pick arms, legs, cardio, core, full body or mobility, then record the duration and effort that fit your day.

Does my Tandem need their own Duo subscription?

No. One Duo subscription covers you and one Tandem you choose. You can pick your Duo Tandem later.

Does Fitandem record workouts automatically?

No. Watching a workout never records exercise on its own. Fitandem asks whether you want to log a move when you finish.

Is it available on iPhone?

Fitandem is currently available on Android through Google Play.
