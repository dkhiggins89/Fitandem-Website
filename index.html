Frequently asked

A few useful things to know.

Quick answers about using Fitandem with someone in your corner.

Is Fitandem free?

Yes. The complete core accountability experience stays free. Fitandem Duo is an optional subscription for advanced insights, challenges, full history and Shared Streak Rescue.

What counts as a move?

Any movement you choose to log can count. Pick arms, legs, cardio, core, full body or mobility, then record the duration and effort that fit your day.

How does a Shared Streak work?

A Shared Streak grows when both people in a Tandem move on the same day. It is designed to encourage consistency together, without leaderboards or pressure.

Does Fitandem record workouts automatically?

No. You stay in control. Watching a workout never records exercise automatically; Fitandem asks whether you want to log a move when you finish.
