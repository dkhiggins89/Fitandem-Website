Frequently asked

A few useful things to know.

Quick answers about building accountability with someone in your corner.

Do we need to work out at the same time?

No. A Shared Streak grows when you both move on the same day — it doesn't matter whether that happens at 6am or 9pm, together or apart.

What if my Tandem misses a day?

The Shared Streak simply resets rather than penalising anyone. If you want extra protection, Fitandem Duo includes Shared Streak Rescue, available again seven days after use.

Is Fitandem free to use for accountability?

Yes. Tandems, Shared Streak, friendly nudges, progress tracking and achievements are all part of the free core experience on Android.

What counts as a move?

Any movement you choose to log — arms, legs, cardio, core, full body or mobility — with the duration and effort that fit your day.
