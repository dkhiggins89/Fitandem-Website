How it works

Two people, one streak, no punishment.

A Shared Streak is designed to encourage consistency, not competition. Our guide explains how to keep a shared streak helpful when real life interrupts.

01

Both people move

The streak grows only on days when you and your Tandem each log a move — no solo credit.

02

Any real-life movement counts

Arms, legs, cardio, core, full body or mobility — pick whatever fits your day and log the duration and effort.

03

No leaderboards, no losing

There's no ranking against other Tandems, and no penalty beyond the streak resetting if a day gets missed.

04

Progress and achievements

Track your current and best streaks, and unlock achievements for shared milestones along the way.

Frequently asked

A few useful things to know.

Quick answers about how a Shared Streak works in Fitandem.

What counts toward a Shared Streak?

Any movement logged by both you and your Tandem on the same calendar day — the categories, duration and effort can be completely different.

What happens if we miss a day?

The streak resets rather than punishing anyone further. Fitandem Duo's Shared Streak Rescue can restore a recently broken streak and becomes available again seven days after use.

Do we have to do the same workout to keep the streak going?

No. One of you could go for a run while the other does mobility work — as long as you both log a move, the streak continues.

Can we see how our streak is progressing?

Yes. Your Progress screen and Achievements collection show your current and best streaks alongside the milestones you've unlocked together.
